Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

"clipper" is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Trending Abroad YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app uses

The app asks for one Google permission: youtube.upload. It uses that permission only to upload videos to the owner's own Trending Abroad channel.

The app does not read, collect, store, sell or share any personal data, viewing history, contacts or other Google user data. The only credential it keeps is the owner's own access token, which is stored as an encrypted secret and used only for uploads.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Videos are uploaded under the YouTube Terms of Service.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on the channel's About page.
